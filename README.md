## 📝 Overview

Farmers often recognize crop diseases or pest infestations only after visible damage has spread, while laboratory diagnosis and expert advice are not immediately available. Weather, crop stage, variety, soil condition, and local pest history influence risk, but these inputs are rarely combined into actionable farm-level alerts.

This project delivers a farmer- and extension-worker-friendly system featuring:
* **Symptom & Pest Detection:** Image-based identification from leaf photos and pest traps.
* **Risk Forecasting:** Weather and environmental risk modeling.
* **Geospatial Mapping:** Hotspot tracking for regional monitoring.
* **Actionable Advisories:** Multilingual guidance and Integrated Pest Management (IPM) recommendations.
* **Official Dashboards:** Surveillance and intervention planning tools for agriculture officials.
